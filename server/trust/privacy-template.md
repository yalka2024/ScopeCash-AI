## 6. Your privacy rights

If you are a **California resident**, the CCPA (as amended by the CPRA) gives you the right to:
- know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we hold about you,
- request deletion,
- request correction of inaccurate information,
- opt out of sale/sharing (not applicable — we do not sell or share personal information),
- limit use of sensitive personal information,
- not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Residents of other states with comprehensive privacy laws in effect (e.g. Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and others as they take effect) have comparable rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability.

To exercise any of these rights, email `privacy@scopecash-ai.app`. We will verify your identity before acting on the request and will respond within the time required by applicable law.
